Questions, answered

The boring questions.
With less boring answers.

A few useful details before you hit the button.

Do I need an Instagram account?

No. The interface is designed around public URLs and does not ask for Instagram login credentials.

What does ReelSloth support?

Public videos, Reels, photos, and multi-item posts are supported where the source makes the media available.

Can I download Instagram Reels without a watermark?

ReelSloth does not add a watermark to downloaded media. If Instagram makes the Reel source available without an embedded watermark, the downloaded file can retain that source version.

Why might a link fail?

Third-party platforms can change how their pages and media are delivered. A link can also be private, unavailable, or inaccessible to the extraction engine.

Do you store my Instagram password?

No password or Instagram login flow is part of this version.
