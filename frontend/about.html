ABOUT INSTAGRAB

A tiny tool for public media.

InstaGrab is a simple browser-based utility built around one idea: paste a public Instagram link, let the extraction engine find media that is available, and save it without an account or a complicated workflow.

What InstaGrab does

InstaGrab accepts supported public Instagram URLs and asks its backend to retrieve media that the source makes available. Depending on the post, that can include Reels, videos, photos, or multiple items from a carousel.

The interface is intentionally small. There is no feed, account system, social profile, or login wall. The goal is to make a single utility quick to understand.

Built around public links

The downloader is designed for media that Instagram makes accessible through public URLs. Private, restricted, removed, or otherwise inaccessible posts may fail and should not be treated as supported downloads.

What we are not
